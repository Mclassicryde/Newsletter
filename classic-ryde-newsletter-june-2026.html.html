Driver Safety

Stay Cool, Keep Your Passengers Safe

Summer Heat Advisory

AC, Hydration, and Your Most Vulnerable Riders

Summer temperatures are climbing. Before every shift, check that your AC is working. Carry water and stay hydrated yourself.

For elderly NEMT clients, especially dialysis patients, cabin temperature is a real health risk. Heat stroke after dialysis is dangerous and can be fatal. Keep the AC running, never leave a senior passenger waiting in direct sun, and watch for warning signs (confusion, heavy sweating, dizziness, weakness). If a passenger seems unwell, call dispatch at 718-777-7800 right away.

Climatisation, hydratation et vos passagers les plus fragiles

Les températures estivales augmentent. Avant chaque shift, vérifiez que votre climatisation fonctionne. Emportez de l'eau et restez hydraté.

Pour les clients NEMT âgés, particulièrement les patients dialysés, la température de l'habitacle est un vrai risque pour la santé.
