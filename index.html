Driver Safety

Stay Cool, Keep Your Passengers Safe

Summer Heat Advisory

AC, Hydration, and Your Most Vulnerable Riders

July is the hottest month in New York. Before every shift, confirm that your AC is working. Carry water and stay hydrated yourself. Drivers with a reported AC issue will be placed on hold until functionality is verified. Do not take trips with passengers if your AC is not working in this heat.

For elderly clients, especially dialysis patients, cabin temperature is a real health risk. Heat stroke after dialysis is dangerous and can be fatal. Keep the AC running, never leave a senior passenger waiting in direct sun, and watch for warning signs (confusion, heavy sweating, dizziness, weakness). If a passenger seems unwell, call 911 for medical attention first, then call dispatch at 718-777-7800 right away.
